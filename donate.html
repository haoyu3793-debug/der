Where your gift goes

Funds are directed at three priorities

🦌

Deer welfare

Annual health surveys, rut-season monitoring (October), and emergency veterinary care for the ~600-strong fallow herd.

🌿

Habitat conservation

Native woodland regeneration in Furry Glen, grassland management at Fifteen Acres, and pond restoration.

📚

Visitor education

Ranger-led walks, school outreach, and the responsible-watching code (50 m distance, no feeding, no flash).
